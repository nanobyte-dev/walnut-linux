Armagetron Advanced 0.2.9.3 is Debian's package, unpatched, and runs with the right picture and sound.
The test is a local game of four AI cycles that the player watches, round after round, at about 83
frames a second with a 1% low of 24. On a first start the game shows a language menu and a player
setup that wait for a key press.
