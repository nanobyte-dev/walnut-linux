Chromium B.S.U. 0.9.16.1 runs on Walnut's own package, which keeps the playfield in its proper proportion on a 16:9 screen (Debian's build stretched it) and maps the DualShock. A played session held about 56 frames a second (1% low 41) at 1920x1080, with its music and effects and no sound dropouts.
