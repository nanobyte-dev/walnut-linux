# Command & Conquer: Tiberian Dawn (Vanilla Conquer): technical notes

Also known as: Vanilla Conquer (the engine, from EA's official GPL release).

## Engine options

- Vanilla Conquer (the pick: C++, the original game; MorphOS BE prior art)
- OpenRA (a remake with its own rules, .NET: gamedb:openra)
- CnC_Remastered_Collection (EA's official GPL source drop of the game DLLs, archived: feeds Vanilla Conquer)
- Chronoshift (a dead earlier RA port)
