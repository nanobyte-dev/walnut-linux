Flight of the Amazon Queen runs on ScummVM with the free CD version Debian packages (with speech),
installed by Walnut Games; the menu entry starts the game directly, fullscreen with the picture between
bars. It was started once on the PS3 in a smoke test. Nobody has played a session of it on Walnut yet.
