Fate of Atlantis runs on ScummVM, Debian's package, from your own copy: Walnut Games makes a package
of its files (the Overview's Installation, and the Guides), and the menu entry starts the game directly,
fullscreen with the picture between bars. A smoke run on the PS3 with the Spanish floppy release reached
Indy in the college's store room with his first line, colours right. Nobody has played a session of it on
Walnut yet.
