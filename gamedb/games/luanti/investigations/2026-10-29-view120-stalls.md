# Luanti: the stalls at viewing range 120 (2026-10-29)

**Symptom.** At the package's viewing range of 120 (0009; the user's floor, no lower) the bench flight
ran at 20 fps steady with 36 s of frames over 100 ms in its 135 s window, a 1% low of 4 fps and frames
up to 3.8 s (gamedb:batch/2026-10-29-luanti-view120). The stall recorder put two thirds of it on `cpu`
(the main thread in state R) and a quarter on swapping from the VRAM swap.

**What the main thread did.** One run with `STALLS=1 PERF=1 MEM=1` and one with Luanti's own profiler
in a log (recipe variant `prof`). `bench/analysis/stallperf.py` places the perf samples (cpu-clock, on
the monotonic clock) in the recorder's stall frames. CPU seconds per thread:

| Thread | in the 55.9 s of stall frames | in the other 79.1 s |
|---|---|---|
| main (render) | 12.6 s (22%) | 36.2 s (46%) |
| MeshUpdate | 15.8 s (28%) | 29.0 s (37%) |
| kswapd0 | 15.3 s (27%) | 5.0 s (6%) |
| Emerge-0 (map generation) | 13.9 s (25%) | 24.5 s (31%) |
| Server | 7.9 s (14%) | 12.3 s (16%) |

The main thread was runnable but not running: the mesh, map generation and server threads and
kswapd had the two PPE threads. The recorder's `runq` rule only fires when the whole process is
starved, so it said `cpu` (docs/STALLS.md, "What the recorder found in a game: Luanti"). When the main
thread did run in a stall, it drew (renderMap 53%, the driver below it), rebuilt the draw list
(updateDrawList 14%, half of it occlusion raycasts) and took page faults (11%, swap-ins). The merged
mesh buffers, which looked suspicious in the source (re-merged whenever the visible set changes), hit
their cache 77-99% of the time and cost 0.1-1.5 ms a frame.

**Memory.** The process ended the window with 115-128 MB resident and 77-105 MB swapped, malloc
152-177 MB in use. The built-in server shares the process: its log said "4128 blocks in memory",
about 4000 map blocks of 16 KiB (the generated chunks around the flight, kept 29 s after their last
use), against the client's 1000-1300. kswapd's share went from 6% of the time to 27% in the stall
frames.

**The black screenshot** of one 120 run at 100 s (the sky black too, the HUD drawn) is the flight path,
not the renderer. The path runs through a mountain: shots of both builds at 60 and 150 s after the
load (15 s into loops 2 and 3, the same place) are black, the 100 s shot shows the mountain just
ahead, and the warm-up shots at 20 and 30 s are grey with a black hand. Luanti blacks out the screen
when the camera is in a solid node; grey is the camera where no block has arrived yet (the first loop
outruns the map generation), so no sunlight is seen and nothing is drawn. The frames inside the rock
are still drawn behind the black (the profiler log's drawn blocks stay at 100-216 through the window),
a little faster: 21 ms median before the 60 s capture against 28 ms before the 100 s one.

## The fixes

- **luanti 0010: the background threads at nice 10** (`LU_NICE`, default 10; 0 as upstream). Thread
  names MeshUpdate, MinimapUpdate, Server, Emerge-N and AsyncWorker-N set their own nice value in
  `Thread::threadProc` (Linux keeps it per thread; raising one's own needs no privilege). The work is
  the same, done in the render thread's gaps. First tried from outside (recipe `lu_renice`, variant
  `v120nice`, batch luanti-renice): 30 fps against 20, 10.2 s of stalls against 35.1, 1% low 8.4
  against 4.1. In the engine (batch luanti-nice, `v120nonice` the off-switch): 30 against 20 fps,
  10.7 against 30.1 s, 8.5 against 4.2. A second mesh thread (`v120mesh2`) is worse: 28 fps, 12.8 s.
- **luanti 0011: the server unloads unused map blocks after 5 s, not 29.** Nothing visible changes:
  the client keeps its own copy of every block, the blocks around the player stay active, and a
  block the server needs again is read back from the world database. With 0010 in (batch
  luanti-srvunload, 3 rounds): 29 s 31 fps, 9.1-9.6 s of stalls, 1% low 9.0, 80-101 MB swapped at the
  end; 10 s 34 fps, 3.8 s, 13.2, 52-56 MB; 5 s 38 fps, 1.2 s, 16.9, 50-56 MB.

Both together, as built (0011 at 5 s), against both off (`v120old`, the game as ps3.1123 ran it), 3
rounds, the same boot (gamedb:batch/2026-10-29-luanti-shipped):

| | steady fps | 1% low | stalls in 135 s | longest frame | swap stalls | swapped at the end |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before (`v120old`) | 21 (19-21) | 4.3 | 30.9 s (30.8-35.1) | 1409 ms | 8.2 s | 97-105 MB |
| after (`view120`) | 38 (38-39) | 16.7 | 1.8 s (1.3-2.4) | 210 ms | 0.1 s | 49-53 MB |

## What is left

The main thread's own frame at 120 (renderMap's 180 draw calls a frame, updateDrawList's 15-40 ms
four times a second) holds the steady rate near 38 fps; the stalls left are short bursts with
kswapd. Screenshots taken inside the window cost 1-3 s of stalls at this load (xwd makes Xorg copy
the frame out of VRAM while the game swaps): stall numbers want the capture in the warm-up. The
flight path wants fixing (todo.toml): of its shots inside the window most are rock. Not measured: a
played session (pop-in while walking, digging and placing with the server at nice 10), and memory
over a long session, where the client keeps up to 7500 blocks for 10 minutes
(`client_mapblock_limit`, `client_unload_unused_data_timeout`).
