Lure of the Temptress runs on ScummVM with the free release Debian packages, installed by Walnut
Games; the menu entry starts its English version directly, fullscreen with the picture between bars
(German, French, Italian and Spanish are in the same package). It was started once on the PS3 in a smoke
test. Nobody has played a session of it on Walnut yet.
