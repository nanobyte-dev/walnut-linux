# Original graphics and sound

OpenTTD plays with its free OpenGFX, OpenSFX and OpenMSX. If you own Transport Tycoon Deluxe for Windows, its original graphics, sounds and music can replace them. It needs the `trg*.grf` files and `sample.cat` of the Windows version, and takes its music in either arrangement: the General MIDI `gm_tt*.gm` files or the FM synthesizer ones, `fm/9_tt*.fm`, which some copies have instead. The data is installed as `transport-tycoon-deluxe-data`, with `-music` (General MIDI, OpenTTD's `original_windows` music set) or `-music-fm` (the FM files and their own set, `original_windows_fm`, which our game-data-packager writes); both are Standard MIDI files that OpenTTD plays through FluidSynth. Once `trg1r.grf` exists the game offers a launcher with the original sets (`-I`/`-S original_windows`, and `-M` the General MIDI music when it is there, else the FM music); it picks them at every start, so a music choice made in its options lasts until the next start.
