# Setting up the game data

## Which editions work

- **Shareware** (free): version 1.06, the first episode, downloaded as `quake106.zip` by game-data-packager (package `quake-shareware`); a `quake106.zip` you already have works as a source too.
- **The full game**: package `quake-registered`. It needs `id1/pak0.pak` and `id1/pak1.pak` of your copy: a CD, The Offering from GOG (the installed folder or `setup_quake_the_offering_*.exe`), or a Steam `common/Quake` folder. The 2021 re-release can't stand in for it: it has no original paks (it is an edition of its own, below). A CD's 1.01 `pak0.pak` is brought to 1.06 with the patch files game-data-packager downloads.
- **The CD's music**: package `quake-music`, optional. A BIN/CUE image of the CD (give the `.cue`, or the `.bin` next to it) brings the data and the music in one import.
- **The 2021 re-release** (Nightdive's Quake Enhanced): package `quake-rerelease`, edition Remaster. From GOG's installer `setup_quake_enhanced_1.0_(60828).exe` (607 MB) or that build installed; other builds of the re-release (Steam's, older GOG updates) have not been checked. It takes the re-release's own `id1/pak0.pak` (the whole game in one 172 MB pak, with coloured lighting and the re-release's QuakeC), its English text from `QuakeEX.kpf` and its CD music, 257 MB installed. The import on the PS3 takes about four minutes.
- **The re-release's add-ons**, optional, from the same installer: Scourge of Armagon (`quake-rerelease-armagon`, 103 MB with its music), Dissolution of Eternity (`quake-rerelease-dissolution`, 70 MB with its music), Dimension of the Past (`quake-rerelease-dopa`, 39 MB) and Dimension of the Machine (`quake-rerelease-dimension-of-the-machine`, 474 MB). These are the re-release's own builds, with QuakeC written for it, so they need `quake-rerelease` and don't play with the original game's paks. An import of the installer makes the re-release and then names each add-on it holds (Walnut Games shows an Also add button for each; `walnut-data import -p quake-rerelease-dopa quakespasm <installer>` adds one). All four played on the PS3 (2026-10-28), each with its own music where the pack has it. Dimension of the Machine needs about 2.5 GB free while it is made. The installer's CTF pak (Threewave Capture the Flag) is left out: it is for multiplayer, and QuakeSpasm has no bots.
- The mission packs (`quake-armagon`, `quake-dissolution`) and their music are packages of the same tool; they have not been tried on the PS3.

## What you get

The data ends up in `/usr/share/games/quake/id1`, the music in `/usr/share/games/quake/id1/music` (QuakeSpasm plays `trackNN.ogg` there for the CD track a level asks for). The menu entries QuakeSpasm (GLSL) and QuakeSpasm (no GLSL) read `/usr/share/games/quake`, so they play whichever edition is installed, and they show only when one is (shareware or full game). `quake-registered` replaces `quake-shareware`, so the full game takes over the same folder. The 2021 re-release has a folder of its own, `/usr/share/games/quake-rerelease`, next to the original game, and its own menu entry, Quake (2021 re-release), which shows once it is installed; each add-on goes into a folder there (`hipnotic`, `rogue`, `dopa`, `mg1`) and has its own entry, such as Quake (2021): Dimension of the Machine. The original game keeps its saves and settings in `~/.quakespasm/id1`, the re-release in `~/.quakespasm/rerelease` and each add-on in `~/.quakespasm/<its folder>`.
