# Grand Theft Auto III, Vice City and Liberty City Stories (re3): technical notes

Also known as: re3 (GTA III); reVC (Vice City); reLCS (Liberty City Stories); hez-gta-re3 (the fork covering all three).

## Engine options

- hez-gta-re3 (the pick: re3 + reVC + reLCS in one tree, the most maintained fork; 117 stars, 2026-08-31)
- mrxenginner/reVC (a Vice City fork/mirror, the most stars, active)
- OpenRW (a clean-room GPLv3 GTA III engine, incomplete)
- reVCDOS (a browser port)
- GTAmodding/re3, halpz/re3 (removed: HTTP 451, the Take-Two DMCA/suit of 2021)
